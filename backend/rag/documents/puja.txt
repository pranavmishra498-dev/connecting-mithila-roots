# Puja and Religious Traditions of Mithila

Mithila has a diverse religious and spiritual heritage. Religious practices include traditional Hindu rituals, festivals, worship, prayers, and family ceremonies.

Chhath Puja is one of the most important festivals celebrated in Bihar and the Mithila region. Devotees worship the Sun God and offer prayers near rivers, ponds, and other water bodies.

Vivah Panchami is associated with the divine marriage of Lord Rama and Goddess Sita. It has special cultural importance in the Mithila region.

Madhushravani is a traditional festival observed by married women in Mithila. It includes rituals, stories, and worship associated with traditional beliefs.

Traditional wedding ceremonies in Mithila include several rituals, prayers, folk songs, and family customs.

Vedic priests and knowledgeable religious scholars may help families perform religious ceremonies and understand traditional rituals.
