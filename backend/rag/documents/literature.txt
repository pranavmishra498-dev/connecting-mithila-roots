# Literature of Mithila

Mithila has a rich literary heritage, particularly in the Maithili language.

Maithili literature includes poetry, songs, stories, drama, essays, religious writings, and modern literature.

Vidyapati is one of the most famous poets associated with Maithili literature. His poetry is especially known for devotional and romantic themes.

Jyotirishwar Thakur is another important literary personality associated with Mithila. His work Varnaratnakara is considered an important early prose work in Maithili.

Maithili folk literature includes traditional songs, stories, proverbs, and oral traditions passed from one generation to another.

Traditional wedding songs, seasonal songs, devotional songs, and folk narratives are important parts of Mithila's literary heritage.

Modern Maithili writers contribute to poetry, novels, short stories, criticism, and contemporary social discussions.

Books and literary resources help younger generations understand Maithili language, history, and culture.

Preserving Maithili books and encouraging reading are important for protecting the literary heritage of Mithila.
