Holi, Diwali and Makar Sankranti.# Festivals of Mithila

Mithila celebrates many traditional, religious, and seasonal festivals. These festivals reflect the cultural identity, beliefs, and community traditions of the region.

## Chhath Puja

Chhath Puja is one of the most important festivals in Bihar and Mithila. Devotees worship the Sun God and offer prayers to the setting and rising Sun. The festival includes fasting and offerings near water bodies.

## Sama Chakeva

Sama Chakeva is a traditional festival celebrated in the Mithila region. It is associated with the relationship between brothers and sisters and is celebrated with folk songs, clay figures, and community activities.

## Vivah Panchami

Vivah Panchami celebrates the traditional account of the marriage of Lord Rama and Goddess Sita. The festival has special significance in Mithila because of its association with the kingdom of King Janaka.

## Madhushravani

Madhushravani is a traditional festival observed by newly married women in Mithila. It includes worship, traditional stories, and cultural rituals.

## Holi

Holi is celebrated with colors, music, sweets, and social gatherings. It represents joy and the arrival of spring.

## Diwali

Diwali is celebrated with lamps, prayers, decorations, and family gatherings.

## Makar Sankranti

Makar Sankranti marks the Sun's transition into Capricorn according to the traditional Hindu calendar. It is associated with seasonal food and cultural celebrations.

Mithila's festivals help preserve its folk traditions, family relationships, religious beliefs, and community identity.
