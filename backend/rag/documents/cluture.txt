# Culture of Mithila

Mithila is a historical and cultural region located in northern Bihar, India, and adjoining parts of Nepal.

Mithila has a rich cultural heritage, including traditional art, music, literature, food, festivals, and social customs.

Maithili is the traditional language of the Mithila region. It plays an important role in preserving the identity and cultural heritage of the people.

Madhubani painting is one of the most recognized traditional art forms of Mithila. It is known for its colorful patterns, geometric designs, and depictions of nature, mythology, and everyday life.

Traditional Mithila weddings include several cultural rituals, songs, and customs. Wedding songs are an important part of the region's folk traditions.

Paan, makhana, curd, and traditional sweets are associated with Mithila's food culture.

Mithila culture values family relationships, hospitality, traditional knowledge, folk arts, and community celebrations.

The cultural heritage of Mithila is preserved through its language, festivals, paintings, literature, music, and traditional practices.
